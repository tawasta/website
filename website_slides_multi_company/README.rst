========================
eLearning: Multi Company
========================

* Adds a company to eLearning courses. Courses without a company are shared
  with all companies.
* Course contents, attendees, content progress, quiz questions, quiz answers
  and additional resources get the company of their course.
* Multi-company record rules limit all of the above to the companies selected
  in the company switcher.
* The website of a course must belong to the company of the course. Selecting
  a website sets the course company to the company of the website.

Configuration
=============
* On install, existing courses get the company of their website. Courses
  without a website are left without a company, so they stay visible in all
  companies.
* Check the company of the courses under eLearning > Courses.

Usage
=====
* Set the company on the course form under Options > Course.
